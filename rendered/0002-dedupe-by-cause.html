0002 · Duplicates share a cause

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Deciders: support desk · Replaces: 0001

Context

Decision 0001 merged reports on subject alone. In #4471 that put reports about different bugs into one ticket and marked it known. Read against each report’s tenant and channel, the ticket held two problems, parser and hint, each with its own fix. See the split.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they share a cause, as causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts gives it from the tenant’s locale, the tenant’s booking form and the report’s channel. The subject line and the intake tag are not evidence: intake tagged what it saw in the reporter’s browser, and the tag disagrees with the cause. Reports the desk read differently (desk-notes.json) stay where they are filed and are footnoted.

Consequences

  • One ticket per cause, each with its own count. A fix closes only the ticket it covers.
  • Triage reads the tenant and channel, not only the subject. That costs a lookup per report.
  • Decision 0001 stays as written. This record replaces it.
During an incident, when the cause is not clear yet, merge on subject as a stopgap and re-split once the cause is known. Count again after the split.
